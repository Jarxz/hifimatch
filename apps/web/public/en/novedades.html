What's new / Feature history

The site keeps growing.

A real record of what got added and when — no promises about what's coming, only what already works.

My systems

September 2026

Save, open and delete complete configurations (equipment, room, materials, listening level) with no account needed. It stays only in each person's own browser; see Privacy for the detail.

Message wall

September 2026

A new screen to leave a public message, visible to any visitor of the site — with no prior moderation in this first version. The email requested alongside the message is never shown.

Online equipment search

September 2026

When a piece of equipment isn't in the curated catalog, it can be looked up by brand and model: the site searches the web, extracts the specs with a cited source, and hands them over with confidence explicitly marked as low — never presented as an already-verified fact.

Augmented reality

August 2026

"View in AR" overlays the room plan's already-calculated reflections on the phone's camera, anchored to the real room with a two-tap calibration — available on Android with Chrome (WebXR); there's no equivalent no-install technology on iPhone yet.
